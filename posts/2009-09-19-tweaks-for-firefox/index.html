Tweaks for Firefox

September 19, 2009 One-minute read
Personal • Technology

I tweaked Firefox on my laptop since I didn’t like the default look and behaviour.Here’s some of the changes I made :-a. The Silvermel theme. It makes Firefox look … umm … really different. :) It can also be applied to Thunderbird as well. b. I hated the way Firefox populates 12 maxcomplete results whenever I typed in a URL. It took up all the real-estate screen space, leaving nothing else to view on my small laptop screen. To reduce that, I changed browser.urlbar.maxRichResults to 3 under about:config in Firefox.c. Entered a master password to prevent people from opening the Options settings and seeing my passwords.
