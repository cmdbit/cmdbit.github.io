Rant on laptops

April 18, 2019 2-minute read
Technology

This is more of a rant on the current state of laptops than anything else.

I have been on the market for a new laptop ever since my old one gave up the ghost. My specifications are quite specific and in no order of importance :-

  • Not have a U series processor
  • 16GB of RAM
  • 1920x1080 screen resolution on a 14" or larger screen
  • Removable battery
  • Doesn’t have to be super-thin
  • Easily repairable
  • Can run OpenBSD without much configuration

The U series processor eliminates the majority of laptops nowadays as Intel seems to be optimising their CPU architecture for mobile platforms. I prefer the Quad Core processors of yesteryears which run just as fast as their existing counterparts today.

16GB of RAM is not really a necessity but a like to have. I run OpenBSD with X11 and i3 only so the pressure on memory is low. top records only 11GB of RAM free.

Anything higher than full HD is a strain on my eyes. I have used full HD on a 11 inch screen with disastrous results.

Because of the non-removeable battery requirement, the Apple laptops immediately get the boot. I am not even thinking of the issues with the butterfly keyboard on the current Air and Pro models.

Points 5 to 7 are important to me as I like to swap out damaged components easily. The Lenovo laptops which have a reputation of running OpenBSD perfectly seem to be going in the opposite direction with the release of their T490.

Why does choosing a laptop have to be this hard?
