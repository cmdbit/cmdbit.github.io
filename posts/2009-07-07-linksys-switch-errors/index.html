Linksys switch errors

July 7, 2009 3-minute read
Technology • Business

I was trying to fix this Linksys SRW248G4P switch that malfunctioned yesterday at a customer site. The switch was functioning at the access layer of the site, and luckily the site did have a spare switch, which was promptly hooked up. I hooked up to the faulty switch via a RS232 console cable, set my HyperTerminal to 38400/N/1 and no flow control. And got the following results. And it stuck at this point. Sigh. Since the problem wasn’t with the boot file, I suspected that the runtime image, SRW224G4P_SRW248G4P_image.bix was corrupted. sigh I could see then that there was no point in trying further. The cause of the problem could be anything, ranging from a blown capacitor to a faulty memory chip or EEPROM, let alone a corrupted runtime firmware. So off it goes to RMA.Frankly, I have never thought highly of Linksys switches used in production environments. The minimum I would go for is 3Com, and the best for any job would be the Cisco boxes. But these Linksys boxes are cheap; they go for less than half of the Cisco boxes.I guess you get what you pay for.
