Tools of a Linux / Unix Engineer

March 24, 2009 One-minute read
Technology • Business

Recently, I had an opportunity to take stock of the software tools I use in my daily professional life. Here are some of them …a. VMware - Indispensable in my line of work. I use VMware constantly to try out new OSes or to do demo installations for customers. Other alternatives include VirtualBox, Qemu, KVM and Xen.b. Putty - As Windows does not have any in-built ssh clients, I use Putty constantly to access *Unix terminals. It’s free, small and does the job without any fuss.c. WinSCP - SFTP/SCP file transfers have never gotten easier. Works for FTP as well. I was first introduced in my previous job, and I have been using it ever since.d. Cygwin - I needed a Bash terminal on my Windows machines to cure my need for a terminal window. :) and so Cygwin fits the bill nicely. It’s nice to see Windows with that ‘Linux-y’ look and feel, although my colleague (who is a FreeBSD purist) disagrees with me completely.
