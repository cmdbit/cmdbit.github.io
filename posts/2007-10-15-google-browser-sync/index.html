Google Browser Sync

October 15, 2007 One-minute read
Technology

For those of you who switch between different computers all the time, you may have found it frustrating to transfer your browser settings. Fear not, Google has a utility to help you do exactly that. This plugin works only for Firefox. It uses your account at Google to help store your cookies, bookmarks, history and if you choose, even your saved passwords. You can choose to encrypt the data being transferred to Google as well. Google Browser Sync is completely automated. The settings you select at startup are automatically synchronized across each of the computers on which you install Browser Sync. You won’t even need to log in every time you start the browser. You can change which browser components are being synced – or even stop the syncing process entirely – using the settings panel in the upper-right corner of the page. The settings panel also gives you access to your PIN.
