SSL certificates

December 17, 2012 One-minute read
Technology

I applied for a SSL certificate for my domain via startssl.com. The application was painless and 2 hours and 6 emails later to verify my address and identity, I was issued an SSL certificate signed by Startcom PKI. From then on, it was a simple matter to copy the SSL certificate into my webserver. SSL certificates are a good way for you to verify your identity to people browsing your websites. The connection is also encrypted which means sniffers and people potentially capturing packets of your session cannot do so.
